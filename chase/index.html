Controls

Hold the phone flat like a tray. Tip the left or right edge down to turn; the further you tip, the faster you turn. Tip the top edge away from you to walk forward, toward you to walk back. Level means stop.

Turn your body to turn. Hold the movement pad to walk: drag toward you to walk back, away to walk forward. Let go to stop.

Double tap and hold the movement pad to start walking. Drag left or right to turn; the further you drag, the sharper the turn. Drag toward you to walk back, away to walk forward. Keep holding to keep moving; let go to stop.

Movement pad

Walk: swipe up to step forward, down to step back. Turn: swipe up to turn right, down to turn left. The buttons do the same.
