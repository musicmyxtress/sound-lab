Controls
Movement pad

Turn toward the mystery sound and walk to it. Headphones work best. Lock portrait orientation.

Tilt: hold the phone flat. Tip left or right to turn, top edge away to walk, toward you to back up.

Hold and drag: double tap and hold the pad to walk. Drag left or right to turn, toward you to back up, away to go forward. Let go to stop.

Compass: turn your body to turn. Hold the pad to walk; drag toward you to back up.

Steps: made for braille displays and keyboards. Use the four buttons, or adjust Walk and Turn up or down.
