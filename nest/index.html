    About this lab

    A first taste of Tuneling Nest. Hatch an egg, name your Tuneling, and feed it. Every Tuneling secretly loves, likes, dislikes or hates each food, and you only find out by feeding it. Loved and liked foods cheer it up and bring you closer. Disliked food gets eaten, grumpily. Hated food gets spat out. Meaty foods slowly make it bigger with a lower voice; carrots and fruit make it smaller and higher. "Leave and come back later" pretends time has passed.

    Songs: hum a tune after the beep and the page picks out the notes (the recording itself is never kept). Save it to the tune book, then sing it to your Tuneling. Each lesson they get it a little more right; three lessons and they're sleepy until you come back. Tunes they've been taught sometimes turn up at the end of happy calls.
